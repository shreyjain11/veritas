# Scientific claims policy

| Claim | Evidence required | Allowed wording | Prohibited overclaim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exact reference overlap | verified corpus match | "reference overlap was observed" | "the model trained on this item" |
| Surface-form sensitivity | paired validated transform effect | "sensitive to canonical surface form" | "proves contamination" |
| Exposure signal | calibrated controls and compatible access | "consistent with exposure" | "proves training membership" |
| Fresh-set decline | difficulty-controlled fresh comparison | "does not transfer to this fresh set" | "the headline score is false" |
